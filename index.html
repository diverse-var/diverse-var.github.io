TL;DR

DiverseVAR is a training-free test-time framework that increases per-prompt diversity in text-conditioned visual autoregressive models while preserving image and video quality. It combines text-embedding noise injection with scale-travel, a multi-scale latent refinement step that recovers visual fidelity from diverse but degraded samples.

Abstract

We introduce DiverseVAR, a test-time framework that enhances the output diversity of text-conditioned visual autoregressive models (VAR) without additional training or substantial computational overhead. While VAR models have recently emerged as strong competitors of diffusion and flow models for image and video generation, they suffer from a critical diversity limitation: even simple prompts often produce nearly identical outputs.

We address this limitation in two stages. First, we systematically explore diversity enhancement techniques by injecting noise into different components of VAR at test time, finding that noise injection into the text embedding yields the best diversity gains. However, this comes at the cost of sharp degradation in image quality. To recover quality, we propose scale-travel: a latent refinement technique that leverages a multi-scale autoencoder to extract coarse-scale tokens and resume generation from intermediate stages.

Extensive experiments demonstrate that combining text-embedding noise injection with scale-travel refinement substantially improves diversity while minimizing quality degradation, advancing the diversity-quality Pareto front.

Method

DiverseVAR follows a diversify-then-refine pipeline. It first weakens the dominant text condition during early VAR generation to unlock multiple modes, then uses the multi-scale structure of VAR to rebuild clean samples from coarse tokens.

1. Inject Diversity

VAR generation is strongly governed by the text condition, which can collapse different random seeds into highly similar outputs. DiverseVAR perturbs the text embedding during early scales, encouraging the model to explore different plausible visual modes for the same prompt.

2. Refine by Scale-Travel

After condition annealing generates a diverse sample, scale-travel maps it back into the multi-scale latent representation. DiverseVAR keeps the coarse tokens that capture global semantics, returns to an intermediate scale, and regenerates later scales to repair local artifacts without collapsing the new variation.
